Faithful paraphrase of the accessible page:
- Submit using ACM Manuscript Central.
- Submit a full paper with appropriate citations.
- The submission must include a cover letter documenting any prior thesis, workshop, conference, or journal version of the material.
- Prepare the manuscript in ACM LaTeX or Word style.
- Implementations, test programs, and source data may be included as supplemental material in the ACM Digital Library if the submission is accepted.

The accessible page did not establish the current TOPLAS anonymity procedure or every live portal field. Those details remain a pre-submission recheck item.
