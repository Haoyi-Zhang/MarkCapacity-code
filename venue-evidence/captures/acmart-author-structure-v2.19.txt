Relevant author-structure conclusions:
- Use one independent author record per person.
- A shared affiliation is repeated per author.
- Institution, city, and country are structured affiliation fields.
- Place \correspondingauthor immediately after the corresponding author's name; the corresponding author must have an email.
- ORCID may be attached to each author with \orcid.
